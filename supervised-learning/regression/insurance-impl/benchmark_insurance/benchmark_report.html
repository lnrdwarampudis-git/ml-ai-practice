Metric guide:
R² — proportion of target variance explained. Primary selection metric. Scale-invariant.
RMSE — root mean squared error in USD. Penalises large errors (e.g. catastrophic misses on smokers).
MAE — mean absolute error in USD. Most interpretable for actuaries and product managers.
MedAE — median absolute error. Robust to the bimodal distribution (smoker/non-smoker cost gap).
Bias (residual mean) — positive = model systematically underestimates charges; negative = overestimates.
Interval coverage — ±1×RMSE should contain ≈68% of errors for normally distributed residuals.

Statistical tests: Friedman χ² + pairwise Wilcoxon vs champion + Bonferroni correction (α=0.05).
Ethics note: Do not use sex or region as individual pricing factors. Smoker status should comply with applicable insurance regulations.

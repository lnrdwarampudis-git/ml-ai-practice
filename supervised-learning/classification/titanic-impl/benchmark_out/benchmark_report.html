Metric guide:
ROC-AUC — discrimination across all thresholds, threshold-independent.
Average Precision — area under P-R curve, robust to class imbalance.
F1 — harmonic mean of precision and recall, balance between both error types.
Brier score — mean squared error of probabilities (lower = better; 0 = perfect).
MCC (Matthews) — correlation between predicted and actual; robust to imbalance.
Cohen's κ — agreement corrected for chance.

Statistical tests: Friedman χ² (global H₀: all models equal) + pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank vs champion with Bonferroni correction (α = 0.05).
Threshold selection: Youden's J statistic (maximises sensitivity + specificity).

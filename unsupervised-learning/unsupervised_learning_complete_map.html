Complete unsupervised learning curriculum map showing 5 tiers — clustering, density estimation, dimensionality reduction, generative models, and self-supervised learning — with datasets, concepts, and progression paths

Why unsupervised after supervised? Every concept below connects back to what you already built. Clustering = classification without labels. Dimensionality reduction = feature engineering without a target. Density estimation = the probability model underneath Naive Bayes. Generative models = learning the data distribution your regressors assumed but never modelled.
